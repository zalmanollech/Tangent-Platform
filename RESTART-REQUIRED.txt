REFRESH REQUIRED
================

The insurance features have been added but require a SERVER RESTART to appear.

To see the changes:
1. Stop the Tangent Platform service (Ctrl+C in its terminal window)
2. Restart it: npm start
3. Refresh your browser

You should now see:
- "🛡️ Insurance Opportunities" button in Admin Dashboard
- Insurer role works in signup
- Insurer signup redirects to insurance dashboard
